# Project Timeline

## Initial Planning

At the beginning of the project, the team estimated that the project would require approximately **2 to 3 weeks** to complete.

The initial timeline included time for understanding the requirements, implementing the main features, integrating the different components, testing, debugging, and preparing the final documentation.

## Development

The project progressed significantly faster than initially estimated.

During the development phase, both team members spent approximately **24 hours working together continuously on the project**. This intensive development session allowed us to implement and integrate a substantial part of the project in a short period of time.

After this initial intensive session, the remaining work focused mainly on completing the implementation, resolving issues, validating the results, and finalizing the project.

## Actual Timeline

| Phase                          | Planned       | Actual                                                  |
| ------------------------------ | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Requirements analysis          | Week 1        | Completed during the initial phase                      |
| Project setup and architecture | Week 1        | Completed during the initial phase                      |
| Core implementation            | Week 1–2      | Completed significantly earlier than initially expected |
| Integration                    | Week 2        | Completed during development                            |
| Testing and debugging          | Week 2–3      | Completed before finalization                           |
| Documentation                  | Week 3        | Completed during the final phase                        |
| Final project                  | End of Week 3 | **Completed**                                           |

## Timeline Adjustment

The original **2–3 week estimate** was used as a planning reference rather than a strict deadline.

The intensive 24-hour development session resulted in substantial progress and allowed the team to complete many tasks earlier than initially expected. The remaining time was used to improve the implementation, test the project, fix issues, and finalize the documentation.

## Final Status

The project was successfully completed.

The actual development process demonstrated that the initial estimate was conservative, as a significant amount of implementation and integration work was completed during the intensive development session.

The timeline was therefore adapted according to the team's actual progress rather than strictly following the original estimate.
