# Team Organization

## 1. Team Structure

The project was developed by two team members:

* **Denys**
* **Danilo**

The development responsibilities were divided between the two members according to the main components of the project.

Each member was responsible for implementing and testing their own changes before submitting them for review.

---

## 2. Development Workflow

The team followed a simple collaborative workflow:

```text
Development
    ↓
Individual testing
    ↓
Review by the other team member
    ↓
Corrections if necessary
    ↓
Merge into main
```

Each team member worked primarily on their assigned components and was responsible for making sure their changes were working before asking the other member to review them.

---

## 3. Code Review

Before changes were merged into the `main` branch, they were reviewed by the other team member.

The review process was used to:

* Check whether the implementation worked as expected.
* Identify possible bugs.
* Verify that the changes did not break existing functionality.
* Check whether the code was compatible with the rest of the project.
* Discuss possible improvements or restructuring.

If an issue was identified during the review, the developer responsible for the change corrected it before the merge.

---

## 4. Testing Responsibility

Testing was performed by the developer who implemented the change before requesting the review.

After that, the other team member also evaluated the change as part of the review process.

This provided two levels of verification:

1. **Developer testing** — verifying that the new functionality worked.
2. **Peer review/testing** — verifying the change from another perspective and checking its integration with the rest of the project.

---

## 5. Integration into Main

Changes were only integrated into the `main` branch after the other team member had reviewed them.

The `main` branch therefore represented the version of the project that had passed the team's review process.

This approach helped prevent unreviewed changes from being directly integrated into the final version.

---

## 6. Collaboration Principle

Although responsibilities were divided between Denys and Danilo, both members participated in reviewing the project's changes.

The team therefore followed the principle:

> **Each developer implements and tests their changes, the other developer reviews them, and only then are the changes merged into `main`.**

This allowed the team to work independently while maintaining shared responsibility for the quality and integration of the final project.
